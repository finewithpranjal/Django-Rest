Serializers : In DRF, Serializers are responsible for converting complex data such as querysets and models instances to native python datatypes (called serialization)
that can be easil renered into JSON, XML or other content types which is understandable by front end. and it's vice-versa is called deserialization


Complex Data --->| Serializers | ---> Python Data ---> (JSON, XML) ----> | Decrialization | ----> Complex Data

for Decrialization some functions\modules are use
